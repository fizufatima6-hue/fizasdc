About the Department Overview

Founded in 1985, the Department of Computer Science offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes. Our faculty and students work across artificial intelligence, networking, software engineering, and human–computer interaction.

We are committed to rigorous teaching, impactful research, and an inclusive community where every student can thrive.

Programs Offered Academics

B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering

Undergraduate · 4 years · 60 seats

A rigorous foundation in algorithms, systems, databases, and software engineering, with electives in AI, security, and cloud computing.

Programme details

M.Tech in Data Science

Postgraduate · 2 years · 30 seats

Advanced study in machine learning, big-data systems, and statistical inference, culminating in a research thesis.

Contact admissions

Ph.D. in Computer Science

Doctoral · 3–5 years · Rolling admissions

Research-driven programme spanning core CS and interdisciplinary collaborations with engineering and the sciences.
